Lance Reddick

June 7th, 1962 - to March 17, 2023

Lance Reddick (1962–2023) was an American actor and musician whose career connected acclaimed television, action films, and video games (Television Academy). His best-known performances included Cedric Daniels in "The Wire," Phillip Broyles in "Fringe," and Charon in the "John Wick" films (Associated Press / Chicago Sun-Times).

Biography highlights

  • Who he was: Born and raised in Baltimore, Reddick studied music before pursuing acting and graduating from Yale's drama school in 1994 (Los Angeles Times). He described himself as both an actor and a songwriter, rather than choosing between those identities (HoboTrashcan interview).
  • What he did: He became known for controlled, commanding performances, especially as officials navigating difficult institutions: Daniels in "The Wire," Broyles in "Fringe," and Irvin Irving in "Bosch" (Associated Press / Chicago Sun-Times). His voice work included Commander Zavala in "Destiny" and Sylens in the "Horizon" games (Television Academy).
  • Why it mattered: His performance in "The Wire" contributed to a series widely recognized for making television crime drama more complex through its treatment of policing, education, and urban institutions (The New York Times). His connection with audiences extended beyond television: after his death, "Destiny 2" players gathered around his character to pay their respects.

    His death prompted tributes from colleagues and fans across entertainment (Los Angeles Times, GamesRadar+).
